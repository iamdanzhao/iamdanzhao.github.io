I focus on antitrust, consumer protection, and infrastructure. I'm currently pursuing a J.D. at Harvard Law School.

During law school, I've spent time with the New Jersey Attorney General's Office and the Port Authority of New York and New Jersey.

Prior to law school, I was an advisor to Federal Trade Commission Chair Lina Khan. In a past life, I was a data scientist, including a brief stint at Facebook and as a Civic Digital Fellow at the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics.

In my spare time, I like to take photos and explore airports. I've also been building a set of tools to make it easier to read cases, access legislative materials, and conduct legal research.
